THE RULE THAT CONNECTS IT ALL

One simple relationship.
A universe of difference.

In a vacuum, every electromagnetic wave travels at the speed of light. As frequency rises, wavelength gets shorter — and the energy of each photon increases.

THE WAVE EQUATION
c = f × λ

Not all radiation
behaves the same.

The word “radiation” covers a wide range of energy. A key distinction is whether individual photons have enough energy to ionize atoms.

↗

Enough to excite or heat.
Not enough to ionize.

Radio waves, microwaves, infrared and visible light are non-ionizing. At sufficiently high intensities, some can still heat tissue or damage eyes and skin.
